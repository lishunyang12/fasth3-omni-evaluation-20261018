FastH3: BF16 Attention
Provider Evaluation

We compare NVIDIA cuDNN and FastVideo attention kernels while keeping the original BF16 checkpoint, video decoder, conditioning inputs, prompts, and seeds fixed. The evaluation separates end-to-end generation performance, attention latency, and qualitative behavior across three reference-conditioning modes.

Experimental setup. Four RTX PRO 5000 Blackwell 72 GB GPUs over PCIe. HSDP, Ulysses degree 4, text-encoder tensor parallelism 4, and VAE patch parallelism 4. Eager execution, no CPU offloading, and one request at a time.

FastVideo uses its portable Triton block-sparse kernel on SM120, preserving logical tile128 routing through tile64 compute blocks. cuDNN uses native tile128. FlashInfer CAKE scope
The evaluated FlashInfer fork provides an SM120 CAKE Sage kernel with INT8 Q/K and FP8 V. Its SM120 BF16 entry dispatches to CuTe DSL. CAKE Sage is treated as a separate quantized-attention configuration; it is excluded from this comparison, which holds BF16 arithmetic fixed.
