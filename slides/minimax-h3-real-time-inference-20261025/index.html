v1.24 embeds BF16 Reference, Online NVFP4 from the original BF16 weights, and published QAD NVFP4 on one slide. Slide 10 includes the recorded request times and measured LPIPS / SSIM against BF16 across all 124 frames. The browser comparison plays all three videos; the PDF contains still frames. Pages 11–13 explain the public Omni QAD training implementation.

Request times are single observations with different warmup, encoder and HSDP settings. Quality metrics describe this single checkpoint comparison; they do not isolate DiT QAD training. Communication results remain controlled microbenchmarks: FI RDMA is 2.33–2.47× faster than the tested local NCCL implementation. Installed xGMI capacity is unverified, and no communication-only result is presented as an E2E speedup.
