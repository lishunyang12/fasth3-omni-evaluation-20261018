Within NCCL, DiT collectives contribute 116.074 s per GPU, and encoder/VAE/other collectives contribute 6.973 s. Within DiT compute, attention contributes 45.309 s and GEMM/MLP contributes 39.192 s.

Approximately 36.9 s per GPU of the 38.5 s staging/layout bucket is DiT host-to-device transfer, overwhelmingly AdaLN projection weight staging on cache misses. Ordinary attention, MLP and QKV weights remain resident after initialization. The remaining roughly 1.5 s covers device layout and other classified copy work.

Keep these accounting views separate. GPU operation time is not an E2E critical-path decomposition. NCCL kernel residency includes synchronization and peer waiting, and cannot be read as wire transfer time or converted directly to achieved bandwidth.

How the baseline leads into the optimization story

Use the baseline to introduce three measured costs: model computation, communication, and AdaLN staging/layout. Then use slide 18 for the cumulative configurations and their actual endpoint measurements. The current final median is 5.418 s at the same 960 × 544 output geometry, with eight QAD evaluations and its own runtime recipe; the sub-5 s target remains unmet.

The lower-resolution queue has been recovered onto /tmp and is waiting for eight idle GPUs. New resolution results will be reported as explicit tradeoffs after measurement and output review.

Evidence and reproducibility

The matched profile passed eight-device, expected kernel-count, NVTX attribution and exclusive-accounting gates. Independent nsys stats kernel and memory totals match the SQLite analysis exactly.
