What to inspect: small figures across the crowd, clothing and merchandise textures, lantern edges, stable storefronts, and details revealed after pedestrians pass. Use the shared frame slider or slow playback to inspect corresponding times.

Controlled settings: all three clips were newly generated with the same prompt, one reference image, seed, sampler, single GPU and layerwise CPU-offload configuration. All use the same original BF16 encoder, cuDNN BF16 attention and original BF16 video/audio VAE. QAD uses its published DiT weights; its quantized encoder is not used here.

Direct Online NVFP4: untouched BF16 source weights use round-to-nearest NVFP4 with weight tensor amax. Every DiT projection computes its activation global scale from its current input amax; per-16 E4M3 scales are dynamic. No calibration table, QAD weights, QAD training or learned QAD numerical artifacts are used in that arm.

*Timing: one first request after each model starts, including CPU offload, possible first-use JIT, generation, encoding and receipt. Model loading and offline weight packing are excluded. These are not the previous four-GPU production measurements and do not establish quantization speedup.

Metrics: full-resolution decoded frames, aligned by frame index; all 124 frames are averaged. Lower LPIPS and higher SSIM mean closer to this BF16 reference. Independent crowd motion can differ between the outputs. These metrics alone do not assess action correctness, temporal stability, audio or general video quality; trajectories can diverge despite the same seed.

Prompt and reference inputs
Use <Picture 1> as the reference for the entire crowded night-market street, including the dense crowd, rows of shops, hanging lanterns, awnings, illuminated signs and deep street perspective. Preserve the busy wide composition and the realistic photographic appearance.

Goal: a five-second continuous wide shot of a densely populated night market. Keep many distinct pedestrians and shop details simultaneously visible across the foreground, middle distance and background. The street remains full throughout the clip.
Scene: two rows of old shopfronts recede into the distance; warm lantern strings cross overhead; shop lights illuminate counters, merchandise and customers. Preserve the reference's night lighting, varied clothing, shop arrangement and crowded atmosphere. People are distributed at different depths with natural partial occlusions.
0–1.5 s: The camera begins a very slow forward glide at pedestrian eye level. Near the left edge, two shoppers walk past the camera; the central crowd continues walking in both directions. Distant people move independently between the stalls.
1.5–3.5 s: Keep the same uninterrupted wide composition. At a counter on the right, a vendor hands a small shopping bag to a customer. Other people continue browsing and passing behind them. Hanging lanterns sway slightly, while the shop structures remain stable.
3.5–5 s: Continue the slow forward glide. The customer turns into the pedestrian flow with the bag. Foreground pedestrians naturally reveal and occlude the shoppers behind them. The distant street stays densely populated and the rows of lanterns remain visible.
Camera: one continuous eye-level wide-angle shot, moving forward only about one metre over five seconds. Maintain deep focus on the crowd and storefronts, natural perspective and stable exposure. Keep the crowd, shops and lanterns in the same shot throughout.
Sound: continuous indistinct market chatter, footsteps, the rustle of shopping bags and occasional counter clinks. No intelligible dialogue or added music.
Continuity: maintain the layout of the buildings and shops, coherent walking paths, connected limbs and consistent clothing as each person moves. Preserve visible shop signs as scene detail; add no captions, logos or overlay text. No cuts, close-ups, rapid zooms or shallow-depth-of-field portraits.

Reproducible measurements

LPIPS uses pretrained AlexNet with learned v0.1 calibration and FP32 RGB in [−1, 1]. RGB SSIM uses a Gaussian 11 × 11 window, sigma 1.5, population covariance and data range 255. No resizing, registration or frame selection is applied.
