Online NVFP4 in v1.25: original BF16 weights, no QAD training and no pre-calibrated activation table. Each DiT linear computes the global activation multiplier from its current input amax; E2M1 values use per-16 E4M3 block scales. Attention, encoder and VAE retain BF16. The middle video in v1.24 used a pre-calibrated table; its scores are not reused here.

*Timing scope: retained curl request times for these exact videos, including generation, MP4 encoding and receipt, excluding model load and weight packing. One request per variant. Online NVFP4 was the first inference request after startup; retained BF16 and QAD runs used already-active servers. BF16 uses HSDP; NVFP4 variants do not. These values do not isolate a quantization speedup.

Similarity to BF16: all 124 frames, original 1344 × 768 resolution, aligned by frame index. Lower LPIPS and higher SSIM indicate greater similarity to this BF16 clip. A single clip and framewise metrics do not establish overall video quality, temporal quality or audio quality. QAD uses its checkpoint encoder; the other two use a BF16 encoder.

Metric definitions and reproducible data

LPIPS: pretrained AlexNet, learned v0.1 calibration, float32 RGB normalized to [−1, 1], no resizing. SSIM: RGB channel mean, Gaussian 11 × 11 window, σ = 1.5, population covariance, data range 255. Both scores are unweighted frame averages over decoded MP4s.
