From a question to a generated avatar clip

The example uses the recorded self-introduction interaction. The question is translated into English; the displayed reply comes from the LLM preview. The H3 prompt combines that reply with the reference identity, voice and scene instructions. The reference image is the actual input portrait supplied to H3 by the demo server.

The reply preview is intended text, not a verified verbatim transcript of generated speech. No new generation or benchmark was run for this slide.

Full English H3 prompt for this reply
Generate a 5.167-second window of one ongoing avatar conversation, 960 by 544, 24 fps. Keep the reference man with a brown beard, baseball cap, open pale blue shirt and white undershirt, in the same beige room with the ceiling fan and soft indoor lighting. Use a fixed eye-level medium close-up. Inherit the incoming gaze, head pose and hand position from the preceding window.
0.000-0.208 seconds: continue the incoming motion and quiet room tone across the hidden overlap.
0.208-3.541 seconds: The man says exactly this English dialogue in his reference voice: "Hi, I'm Miles. I help with simple, clear answers.". Say it once, with no additional words. His lips follow the words. Keep small natural facial and hand movements.
3.541-5.167 seconds: stop speaking, close the lips and listen quietly. Take a gentle nasal breath with a subtle matching chest movement, ready for the next conversational window.
Keep one speaker and the same identity, clothes, background, camera and lighting. Audio: one dry, close-miked natural speaking voice only during the specified dialogue, then soft unvoiced nasal breathing over a very low steady indoor noise floor. Breathing is much quieter than the dialogue and follows the chest movement. No additional words or whispering. No background music, musical instruments, brass, trumpet, added sound effects, humming or overlapping voices. No scene cut, fade, title or subtitles. Do not repeat the greeting or reset the pose.
Full demo runtime configuration
Hardware8 SM120 GPUs, 73,415 MiB each. Recorded PRO 5000 reference node; driver reports NVIDIA Graphics Device. No PRO 5500 result.
Output960 × 544 at 24 fps. First window: 124 frames / 5.167 s. Each continuation: 119 new frames / 4.958 s after removing 5 overlap frames.
H3 model and precisionRef2VA, 8 evaluations, QAD NVFP4 DiT, native NVFP4 VSA (setting 0.9; reference keep rate 0.1).
Parallelism and communicationTP1 / Ulysses8; text encoder TP8; VAE patch parallelism 8; BF16 RDMA; 2/3/2 head bands.
Decoder and output36-layer NVFP4 video VAE, 216 packed linears, BF16 decode autocast; native H3 audio decoder; MP4 pre-encoding. No separate TTS.
History conditioningVideo and audio latent tails with cumulative AV positions. Video guide: 80% retained latent + 20% seeded noise. Video target prefix off; reference image noise 0.001; audio history remains clean and audio prefix stays off. This conditioning change is not lossless.
Speech detectionSilero ONNX on CPU. Continuous 16 kHz mono PCM; 200 ms packets; 800 ms pre-roll; 600 ms quiet endpoint.
ASRQwen3-ASR-0.6B, BF16, GPU 6 shared with H3. Partial recognition during speech; final recognition at the endpoint.
LLMQwen3-4B, BF16, GPU 7 shared with H3. Thinking disabled; streaming short English replies; temperature 0.4; maximum 160 output tokens.
PlaybackOne active call. At most two unplayed windows, including the in-flight generation. Independent preloading; generation overlaps transfer.
Avatar generationH3 HTTP request through complete MP4; excludes subsequent CPU idle-audio screening and media delivery
