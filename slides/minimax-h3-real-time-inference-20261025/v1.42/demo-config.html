Live avatar: exact configuration

One active microphone conversation, with native H3 audio/video generation and rolling video/audio history. This is the native 8-step demo, not the historical LightX2V 4-step Zhiwei workload.

Hardware8 SM120 GPUs, 73,415 MiB each. Recorded PRO 5000 reference node; driver reports NVIDIA Graphics Device. No PRO 5500 result.
Output960 × 544 at 24 fps. First window: 124 frames / 5.167 s. Each continuation: 119 new frames / 4.958 s after removing 5 overlap frames.
H3 model and precisionRef2VA, 8 evaluations, QAD NVFP4 DiT, native NVFP4 VSA (setting 0.9; reference keep rate 0.1).
Parallelism and communicationTP1 / Ulysses8; text encoder TP8; VAE patch parallelism 8; BF16 RDMA; 2/3/2 head bands.
Decoder and output36-layer NVFP4 video VAE, 216 packed linears, BF16 decode autocast; native H3 audio decoder; MP4 pre-encoding. No separate TTS.
History conditioningVideo and audio latent tails with cumulative AV positions. Video guide: 80% retained latent + 20% seeded noise. Video target prefix off; reference image noise 0.001; audio history remains clean and audio prefix stays off. This conditioning change is not lossless.
Speech detectionSilero ONNX on CPU. Continuous 16 kHz mono PCM; 200 ms packets; 800 ms pre-roll; 600 ms quiet endpoint.
ASRQwen3-ASR-0.6B, BF16, GPU 6 shared with H3. Partial recognition during speech; final recognition at the endpoint.
LLMQwen3-4B, BF16, GPU 7 shared with H3. Thinking disabled; streaming short English replies; temperature 0.4; maximum 160 output tokens.
PlaybackOne active call. At most two unplayed windows, including the in-flight generation. Independent preloading; generation overlaps transfer.
Avatar generationH3 HTTP request through complete MP4; excludes subsequent CPU idle-audio screening and media delivery
Reply video startsEstimated microphone speech endpoint to first rendered reply frame, including endpoint detection, ASR, LLM, queueing, generation, transfer and playback wait
CaptionsRecognized user speech plus intended current reply text; not word-aligned or verified transcription of H3 audio
Benchmark comparisonThe refreshed baseline and retained ladder use fixed prompt/seed on exclusive eight-GPU H3 with ASR/LLM stopped. Later rows were not rerun under the refreshed baseline policy. The live demo shares GPU 6/7 and carries video/audio history.
