What to inspect: small figures across the crowd, clothing and merchandise textures, lantern edges, stable storefronts, and details revealed after pedestrians pass. Use the shared frame slider or slow playback to inspect corresponding times.

Native precision in vLLM-Omni: Online and QAD run the same official OmniRef sparse FP4 kernel. Video fine-attention QK and PV use FP4, with two-level P quantization and K smoothing. The native protected prefix uses FP8 Q/K and BF16 PV. VSA routing, pooling, top-k and the coarse branch keep their higher precision. The language encoder and video decoder use NVFP4 linear layers; the vision tower, audio VAE and designated boundary modules retain higher precision.

Matched request: same prompt, reference image, seed 1234, sampler, resolution and eight evaluations. All use vLLM-Omni with single-GPU layerwise CPU offload. The three quantized clips are generated on GPU 3. BF16 is retained from v1.31 on GPU 0. This is a full deployment comparison; it does not isolate DiT distillation from the separately trained QAD decoder or other quantized components.

QAD: packed checkpoint weights and activation global scales are preserved. The DiT has 100 stored FFN activation globals; the other 250 DiT projection globals are one. Per-16 block scales remain dynamic. The native video decoder uses unit activation globals.

Direct Online NVFP4: weights are independently quantized from original unquantized checkpoints, using RTN, tensor amax and per-16 E4M3 scales. DiT, language encoder and decoder activation globals use current-input amax at every forward. No QAD weights, QAD scale tables, calibration dataset or tuning is used. Sharing the published FP4 attention algorithm does not reuse learned QAD artifacts.

QAD + CAKE Sage: keeps the same QAD DiT, language encoder and video decoder, replacing native fine attention with the existing CAKE Sage path (INT8 Q/K, FP8 V). Its dense prefix follows the CAKE path rather than the native FP8-prefix split. VSA tile128 routing and 90% target sparsity are retained. This fourth arm is an alternative attention path, not an extra FP4 kernel stacked on native attention.

Correction: the v1.30–v1.32 QAD clips used an incorrect dynamic-scale override and non-FP4 attention. They do not represent native QAD. This release regenerates all three quantized clips with the full scope above.

*Timing: one first request after each model starts, including CPU offload, possible first-use JIT, generation, encoding and receipt. Model loading and offline weight packing are excluded. Kernel prechecks populate part of the JIT cache; no full-model warmup was run. These single-GPU offload times do not establish steady-state Sage or quantization speedup, and are not the previous four-GPU production measurements.

Metrics: full-resolution decoded frames, aligned by frame index; all 124 frames are averaged. Lower LPIPS and higher SSIM mean closer to this BF16 reference. Independent crowd motion can differ between the outputs. These metrics alone do not assess action correctness, temporal stability, audio or general video quality; trajectories can diverge despite the same seed.
