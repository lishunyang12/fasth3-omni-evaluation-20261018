Analysis: smaller shard groups are independent of USP8.
For shard size n, BF16 weight storage is W/n per rank and per-request receive payload is 8*(n-1)/n*W. HSDP8: 8.149913 GiB weights and 456.395152 GiB RX; HSDP4 with replicate=2: 16.299827 GiB and 391.195845 GiB; HSDP2 with replicate=4: 32.599654 GiB and 260.797230 GiB; no sharding: 65.199307 GiB and zero HSDP RX. USP activation communication is unchanged.
The HSDP2 mesh uses adjacent pairs (0,1), (2,3), (4,5), (6,7), all PXB according to nvidia-smi topo -m. HSDP4 groups remain inside a NUMA domain; HSDP8 spans NUMA domains. Physical topology does not prove which transport NCCL selects.
Adding only the extra weight storage to the observed HSDP8 reserved peak gives approximately 59.60 GiB for HSDP2 and 43.30 GiB for HSDP4. These are rough capacity estimates, not measured peaks or guarantees. Startup and buffer lifetimes also matter. Neither alternative has been benchmarked. See checkpoint-runs/hsdp-latency-staged-r1/shard-size-tradeoff.json.
Root-only retention is another unmeasured candidate: root weights are 1.486877 GiB (~2.28% of sharded weights), yet root pre-forward intervals total 0.485572 s (~24.85% of exposed waits). Retaining the unsharded root could avoid repeated root aggregation at additional memory cost; it does not imply a measured 0.49 s end-to-end gain. No production changes made.
